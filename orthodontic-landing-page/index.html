Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Give our office a call — we're happy to help.

How do I know if I need braces or Invisalign?
Every treatment plan starts with a free consultation, where one of our doctors evaluates your bite, spacing, and goals to recommend the best option for your lifestyle and budget.
What age should my child first see an orthodontist?
The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an first evaluation by age 7, even if treatment isn't needed right away. Early screening lets us catch issues while there's still room to guide growth.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes — we offer in-house financing with no interest, along with support filing insurance and HSA/FSA claims so treatment fits your budget.
How long does treatment typically take?
Most patients complete treatment in 12–24 months depending on the complexity of their case. We'll give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.
Do you see adult patients?
Absolutely — over a third of our patients are adults. We offer discreet options like clear aligners and ceramic braces designed for professional settings.
